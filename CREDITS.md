# Image credits

All photography in this build comes from [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com) under the
[Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/) (free to use, no attribution required —
credited here anyway as good practice).

**Every image was visually reviewed before being committed**, to confirm it genuinely depicts
an Indian subject or setting and matches the collection it sits in. Photo search metadata
alone was not treated as sufficient evidence.

Source files were downloaded, cropped to a fixed aspect ratio (4:3 for collections, 3:1 for
the hero), resized and encoded to WebP. Originals are not redistributed here.

Each collection has three variants, all sharing the identical crop:

| Variant | Size | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `{slug}.webp` | 400 × 300 | grid thumbnail (1×), search results |
| `{slug}@2x.webp` | 800 × 600 | grid thumbnail (2×) |
| `{slug}-full.webp` | 2000 × 1500 | the Creative Editor source, so download resolutions are real detail rather than upscales |

## Before going live

The Pexels License permits commercial use but asks that you don't redistribute photos
unaltered as stock media. A stock-image marketplace is close enough to that line that the
imagery here should be treated as **placeholder** and replaced with licensed or
commissioned photography before any real commercial launch.
